# PackVault economics: gate, expectation, forward simulation

Generated by `python3 sim/forward.py 400`. Data: `sim/data/fwa.sqlite`, FWA, rewards and oracle events through block 26,425,500 (`python3 sim/ingest.py`); pool snapshot at the end of that history; volume = acquisitions per UTC day that reached a selection (refunded requests distributed no fees). The historical replay and its reconciliation with these numbers are in `sim/out/backtest_audit.md`.

Pool: 2,584 listings, harmonic backing 0.0514 ETH. Successful acquisitions per day: last 7 d 97, last 14 d 71, last 30 d 442 (311 without the 4225 of Oct 31), 30-day median 154.
Vault: 3-unit packs (P = 0.03 ETH) at the 0.0363 cap; k = 1.0385; keeper pay V1 (quoted gas + work fee, no premium); maxTip 0.05 gwei; gas ceiling 0.5 gwei; FWA tokens at zero; purchasers take the bid unless stated (keeps pay the vault more).

FWA staging (audit E7): the keeper lists only when FWA's queue is drained, so a relist never stages; each listing first waits the queue's historical drain time, sampled from 10588 relist moments in the last 30 days (46.4% found the queue busy; waits up to 4.7 h). The forward simulation assumes this; the closed form does not.

## 1. Formal economic gate (owner's definition)

Gate: 10%/yr net of gas at trailing-30-day volume, tokens at zero, at the 0.5 gwei gas ceiling (D42); bid outcome positive at the ceiling; a usable exit window each day. 0.15 gwei is spot at the pinned block. Above the ceiling the keeper waits; calls are still accepted, paid at the ceiling price (D51).

## 5. FWA tokens (burned, never sold; D43)

Holders get no token value by design: the vault claims FWA token rewards and burns them, at the operator's gas cost (`burnTokens`, unpaid). Measured in `keeper/rehearse_canary.py`: one real buyback paid the canary's 28 listings 4.87 FWA tokens (a pool sale would have realized about 8.2e12 wei), all claimed and burned.
