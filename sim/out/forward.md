## 5. FWA tokens (separate from the base case, which counts them at zero)

Measured in `keeper/rehearse_canary.py`: one real buyback (0.0096 ETH budget) paid the canary's 28 listings 4.87 FWA tokens, which sold for 8.2e12 wei. A sale's gas pay at 0.08 gwei was 5.0e13 wei, so the vault does not pay for selling dust and the keeper waits (or an operator subsidizes, as the rehearsal shows). Realized token income is under 0.01% of deposits a year at canary size.
