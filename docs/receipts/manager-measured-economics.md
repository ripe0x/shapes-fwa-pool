# Individual manager: measured reuse-only economics

**Historical A9 two-seed assessment.** Its negative mean is superseded by the [expanded A10 assessment](manager-a10-economics.md). Retain the numbers below only with their original two-path scope and source-bound configuration.

**Decision.** The current 0.03 ETH, three-paid-Shape, reuse-only manager with provisional 0.21 ETH backing does **not** meet a reliable positive owner-return requirement. The two archived month paths average a loss, a quiet path loses its opening costs, and an early-bid burst loses substantially more. The 0.006 ETH reserve is the smaller operational correction for a cash shortfall; it does not make the strategy profitable. No production backing or reserve value is approved by this assessment. The shared vault remains closed.

## Evidence and scope

The [frozen 96-row assessment](../../sim/out/manager-measured-a9-final/summary.json) uses two archived 30-day request windows, one request-keyed path per window, observed buyer choices and a forced-responsive-bid stress, plus quiet and early-burst-then-zero cases. Eight executable cells pair 0.005/0.006 ETH reserve, observed 0.0005/historical accepted 0.001 ETH mint fee, and 0.2/0.5 gwei at fixed 0.21 ETH backing. Every path completed an owner-paid exit within its 0.01 ETH wallet limit; aggregate ledger gaps were zero. These are **conditional model outcomes, not demonstrated APR** or a month of transactions by a deployed manager. Real FWA, Shapes and ShapePacks fork receipts qualify the work gas and selected lifecycle operations; archived demand and buyer response remain assumptions. [Raw receipt profile](manager-measured-cost-calibration.json), [16-step cash reconciliation](manager-measured-reconciliation-a9.json) and [independent review](manager-a9-independent-review.md) state the limits.

## Reserve, opening and keeper limits

The conservative maximum-work keeper need at 0.5 gwei is **0.0055305 ETH**. A 0.005 ETH reserve is short **0.0005305 ETH**; a 0.006 ETH reserve has **0.0004695 ETH** headroom. With the same exposure it asks the owner to leave **0.001 ETH more cash** in the manager. At the observed fee, the one-position funding envelope rises from **0.246925 / 0.2475025 ETH** to **0.247925 / 0.2485025 ETH** at 0.2 / 0.5 gwei, excluding creation wallet gas. All 24 paired reserve comparisons in the completed paths had **zero owner-net difference** because 0.5 ETH funded the same two positions and unused reserve returned at exit. Raising spending permission alone supplies no cash.

The ordinary keeper declined the same small sync context repeatedly in the model. At 0.2 gwei its inherited 0.93 billed-to-estimate ratio made it expect **0.0002192938 ETH** gas against **0.0001520186 ETH** payment. A paired real-counterparty fork receipt for that context billed **501,096 gas = 0.0001002192 ETH** and would have earned a positive margin. The ratio updates only after three successful syncs, so a new manager can wait despite profitable actual work. Other actual sync contexts bill 84–91% of their estimates; lowering the global ratio enough for this context could lose keeper money elsewhere. Owner-paid bounded recovery remains available, but a passive keeper-only operating assumption is unsupported. Calibration-override receipts with a 0.31 gwei payment ceiling are gas-path evidence only; their negative 0.5 gwei keeper margins are not candidate economics.

The 0.03 ETH backing comparison in older work was **hypothetical**: current manager admission rejects that one-to-one backing case. A lower executable backing target would reduce a bid-return loss, but changes admission, buyer incentives and fee earning. It needs a new real-counterparty evaluation rather than subtracting 0.18 ETH per bid from this result. Even zero bid losses cannot make the quiet path earn revenue. The smallest safe present recommendation is to keep the three-mint recipe and reuse intact packs, retain the manager package **unsigned and draft**, and do not claim a profitable default. If the owner chooses to proceed with a speculative personal manager, 0.006 ETH is the better evaluated operating reserve, with extra opening capital and no demonstrated return improvement.

**Things that don't make sense.** The keeper's starting ratio blocks a measured profitable sync before it can learn. Some raw RPC estimates are writer-recorded and four packet writer versions are not preserved, while their billed receipts and work events remain independently checked. A 0.006 ETH reserve covers a maximum-work cash envelope but cannot cover a 0.21 ETH bid-return loss. Historical request activity and synthetic buyer response cannot validate a future owner return.
