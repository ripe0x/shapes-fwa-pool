**Return decision.** The previous −28.5% and later positive 960-case outputs used fixed quoted pay and/or Fresh renewal, so neither measures this runtime. The current model admits the paired one-position contexts and refuses unmeasured larger scans, fee-collection combinations and multi-position sync patterns. A single bounded final-input campaign stopped before its first financial row on actual model work `gasAllowance=780000`, `workUnits=1`; that physical work shape has no matching receipt. No complete, receipt-qualified operating-return distribution or depositor hurdle result exists for this reuse-only manager. It would be false to recommend a production backing setting or claim viability from the opening and exit samples alone. The smallest current design is to keep the fixed 0.03 recipe and reuse returned packs intact; evaluate the provisional backing and overhead after measuring the work patterns actually needed by an operating manager. A fee reduction is not assumed or requested.

**Things that don't make sense.** The 0.005 ETH reserve is **0.0005305 ETH** below the conservative 0.0055305 ETH maximum-work keeper need at 0.5 gwei. The owner must fund real cash or accept a wait; increasing spending permission cannot provide cash. A positive keeper margin on these receipts does not establish a positive owner return, and an unchanged pack supply during reuse creates no new creator mint revenue.
