## Independent asset, authority and economic map

`PackAccount` and the linked strategy libraries operate against each account's own storage. Pooled holders own share claims; personal managers belong to their individual owner. The factory creates/discovers managers and has no continuing withdrawal or economic-setting authority over existing managers.

Pooled spendable ETH excludes gross pending deposits, redemption reserves, distribution reserves, settled claims, processing contributions and finalized contribution refunds. NFT face value, active backing, genuine protocol fee credit and pending fees are distinct assets. Processing/refund cash is a liability, never temporary free capital. Renewal eligibility is single-use authorization, not another NAV asset. Token rewards remain zero-valued and are claimed/burned without paid caller compensation.

For a bid, the backing leaves and the pack returns. Fresh renewal redeems that actual return and buys three paid primary mints plus a new listing. For a keep, the pack leaves and backing less settlement fee returns; one replacement authorization remains. Unselected withdrawal/kick creates no acquisition replacement permission. Exit inventory is redeemed for liquidity without fresh replacement. Round trips reviewed include fee changes then reversal, disabled minting then ordinary relisting, cancelled contributions then restored quotes, partial redemptions, fee credit collection then spending, and complete holder/manager exits.

The local floor `B >= ceil(P / 0.95)` has a distinct accounting purpose: with maximum 5% settlement fee, unresolved keep proceeds cannot fall below the face value used in conservative pricing. It is not an assertion that FWA exposes that floor or that buyers must prefer keeping. The keep-profit condition `B*(k-s) >= P+cost` assesses the account's own outcome. The buyer-preference policy is removed; the provisional `B=.21`, `P=.03` choice itself demonstrates that separation.

Sequencing eras/epochs, acquisition cursors and the distribution high-water mark ratchet by design. Recovery now leaves distribution/pricing rights intact. The new signed maximum reservation also intentionally only increases: lower observed gas, refunds, reverted receipts and reorgs do not replenish the accepted exit allowance. Explicit owner review can raise that allowance. Renewal eligibility and pending/refund liabilities have matching consumption/claim paths.

## Things that don't make sense

- Creator mint revenue can remain positive while depositors lose after construction, shared maintenance and withdrawal costs. The unchanged10% net annual gate was assessed separately and failed here; a conserved ledger or passing test is not economic success.
- Four-draw fee/gas sensitivities have changing state paths. Their counterintuitive ordering does not prove that reducing the fee causes worse returns, and their large positive outliers do not establish reliable holder returns.
- The provisional .21 backing/25bps combination can open a position with less than .5 ETH yet require72 ETH NAV to price during one unresolved allocation. Waiting for actual settlement is material for small holders.
- The retained .0002 checkpoint comparison fails under the current two-gwei essential spike. Adjustability authorizes numerical evaluation, not a claim that historical outcome targets passed or that production economics were approved.
- Tiny refunds can cost more wallet gas than they return. The actual UI outcomes include that expense; refund attribution alone does not make claiming profitable.
- Billed authenticated-VRF renewal/fairness wallet costs and production keystore transport remain missing evidence. The Docker gate pass addresses image execution, not those separate gaps.
