## Asset and authority map

The manager owns its entire ETH balance, inventory, FWA positions and genuine fee credit for one current owner. There are no shares, dead-share seed, depositor checkpoints or contribution/refund liabilities. The immutable factory initializes clones atomically and keeps no withdrawal, setting, owner-change or upgrade authority over existing managers. The current owner retains the existing two-step ownership handover and direct withdrawal rights.

Account funding buys pack principal, separate FWA backing, paid primary mints and quoted keeper work. Normal permissionless keeper compensation consumes this manager's own cash and permissions. A bid can lose the backing while returning the pack; a keep transfers the pack and returns backing less settlement fees. Fresh renewal pays for three new primary mints; reuse relists the returned contents. Eligibility is authorization, not an extra asset.

Guided closeout first enables exit mode, then performs bounded recovery, genuine ready FWA queue processing when needed, unlisting, redemption, final fee collection and withdrawal to the owner. Exit mode stops growth and leaves listings paused if later disabled. Owner-paid maintenance pays no keeper reward. Foreign requesters fund their own requests/refunds; their funds are not counted as manager-owner income or a subsidy. Ending balances were checked against actual holdings and receipts, not only the manager's counters.

The guided wallet limit retains signed maxima across pending, reverted and replacement outcomes. Observed lower gas and incoming withdrawals do not refill it. Creation, funding and separate settings actions are outside that closeout limit but are included in the full owner wallet results below. A wallet's separately signed manual override cannot be undone by the app; detection blocks subsequent steps or requires explicit higher-limit review.

## Things that don't make sense

- Keeper margin and creator fee accrual can be positive while the individual owner loses. The personal manager has no invented10% hurdle, but those losses must remain visible.
- A completed owned book does not require cleaning every foreign journal entry. The failed initial model demonstrated the cost of carrying a pooled assumption into a manager.
- Proxy gas, an optionally excessive processing reserve and two-draw cells cannot support fine-margin profitability promises or a causal claim that one fee/mode is better.
- The UI's productive-funding threshold is stricter than the contract's permitted idle creation. It is a UI limitation, not a new minimum TVL.
- Production keystore/hardware-wallet transport, live service operation, creator withdrawals and billed authenticated-VRF renewal remain untested. The successful local and image campaigns do not grant production authority.
